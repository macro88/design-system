The sheet scrim is a dark wash. Light schemes use 40% opacity and dark schemes use 60%. A light scheme keeps 40% even inside a dark page. The nostr theme uses a neutral near-black, at 40% in light and 64% in dark.

While a sheet is open, a toast sits at the top of a narrow viewport and above the sheet on a wider one, clear of the sheet actions.
